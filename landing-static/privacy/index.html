Privacy Policy

What stays local and what leaves the device.

Desktop-first where possible. Hosted where needed.

Updated March 10, 2026
Draft for final legal review

1. Information we collect

  • Account details such as email, password-derived credentials, and subscription status.
  • Usage and billing events needed to enforce plan limits and manage paid access.
  • Local application data such as settings, window state, and cached task context.
  • Content you intentionally send for processing, such as screenshots, prompts, notes, or browser tasks.

2. What stays local

Depending on the feature, the app may keep local caches, temporary files, screenshots, or note drafts on your computer so actions remain fast and contextual.

3. What may be sent to hosted services

If you use hosted AI features, account login, billing, cloud-backed chat history, or update delivery, relevant data may be transmitted to Sylica infrastructure or third-party service providers needed to deliver those features.

4. Billing and subscriptions

Billing is handled through a payment processor. Subscription status, checkout events, and billing portal activity may still be processed to manage access.

5. AI providers

Sylica AI can route requests to external model providers such as Together AI, OpenAI, Anthropic, or Gemini depending on configuration.

6. Retention and sharing

We keep account, billing, and usage data only as long as needed for product operations, support, fraud prevention, or legal compliance. We do not sell personal information.
