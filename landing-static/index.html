Features

Invisible overlay

Window controls that stay out of the way

Toggle visibility, move the window, and adjust opacity without breaking your current screen setup.
Shortcut-first

Capture and solve in a few key presses

Use Ctrl/Cmd + H to capture, Ctrl/Cmd + Enter to solve, and Ctrl/Cmd + L to remove the last screenshot.
Bring your own models

Choose the provider that fits your stack

Connect OpenAI, Gemini, Anthropic, or Together AI with your own keys and preferred model mix.
Follow-up ready

Debug, refine, and keep iterating

Add more screenshots after the first answer to debug errors, revise code, or explain the next step.
