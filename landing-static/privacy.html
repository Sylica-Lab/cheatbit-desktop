Privacy Policy

What Sylica AI stores locally, what reaches the backend, and what stays on your device.

Sylica AI is desktop-first. Some flows use hosted services for accounts, billing, model access, and updates, while many workflows still rely on data stored on the device. Last updated March 10, 2026.

Local-first Notes, app state, and some task context may stay on the device.
Account-backed Authentication, billing, usage, and sync-linked features use hosted services.
Model providers Some content may be sent to providers you configure for AI processing.
This page is product-specific and should still be reviewed against your final billing, logging, and model-routing policies before broader legal use.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details such as email, password-derived credentials, and subscription status.
  • Usage and billing events needed to enforce plan limits and manage paid access.
  • Local application data such as settings, window state, and cached task context.
  • Content you intentionally send for processing, such as screenshots, chat prompts, notes, or browser tasks.

2. What stays local

Sylica AI is designed to keep as much workflow state on the device as possible. Depending on the feature, the app may keep local caches, temporary files, screenshots, or note drafts on your computer so actions remain fast and contextual.

3. What may be sent to hosted services

If you use hosted AI features, account login, billing, cloud-backed chat history, or update delivery, relevant data may be transmitted to Sylica infrastructure or third-party service providers needed to deliver those features.

4. Billing and subscriptions

Billing is handled through a payment processor. Sylica AI does not need to store raw payment card details directly in the desktop app. Subscription status, checkout events, and billing portal activity may still be processed to manage access.

5. AI providers

Sylica AI can route requests to external model providers such as Together AI, OpenAI, Anthropic, or Gemini depending on configuration. When a feature uses one of those providers, the content required for that feature may be sent to that provider.

6. Retention and sharing

We keep account, billing, and usage data only as long as needed for product operations, support, fraud prevention, or legal compliance. We do not sell personal information. Data may be shared only with service providers needed to run accounts, billing, hosting, model inference, analytics, or support, or when required by law.
